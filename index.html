02 / Intent

Adoption is a change in the system, not a new shortcut.

Faster learning

Use Copilot to explore, explain, draft, transform, and test while keeping engineering judgment in the loop.

Better delivery

Start with a clear outcome and constraints so generated work is connected to a real problem.

Stronger trust

Make review, evidence, ownership, and known limitations visible before work reaches customers.

Our intent:increase capability without lowering the quality bar. The foundation gives the team shared information and standards before hands-on Copilot practice begins.
03 / Adoption mindset

Move from “generate code” to “shape and verify outcomes.”

Old instinct

  • Begin with a code request.
  • Trust a plausible answer too quickly.
  • Discover requirements during implementation.
  • Treat review as a final inspection.

Operating expectation

  • Begin with intent and a problem statement.
  • Ask for evidence, alternatives, and limitations.
  • Make requirements and constraints explicit.
  • Review decisions at the point they matter.
Human accountability remains constant: Copilot can assist with work; accountable people approve it.
