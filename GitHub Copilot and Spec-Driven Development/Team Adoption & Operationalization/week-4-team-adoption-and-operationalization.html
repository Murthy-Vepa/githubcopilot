Team Adoption and Operationalization

Make SDD - Agent Squad a dependable part of delivery by embedding the workflow in ceremonies, quality gates, ownership, support, and measurable improvement.

Week 4Audience: team leads, delivery leads, enablement, and championsFormat: playbook workshop and operating review

Week at a glance

Operationalize

Put SDD in the calendar

Define how specifications, plans, tasks, reviews, and evidence appear in refinement, planning, stand-ups, demos, and retrospectives.

Coordinate

Use SDD - Agent Squad well

Clarify roles, handoffs, checkpoints, escalation paths, and the conditions under which an agent squad should or should not be used.

Improve

Measure the practice

Track adoption and quality signals without turning metrics into a substitute for judgment or a burden that teams cannot maintain.

Learning outcomes

Run team ceremonies

  • Use the specification as a shared reference during refinement and planning.
  • Review task readiness, dependencies, and acceptance evidence.
  • Carry unresolved questions to the right owner instead of hiding them in code.

Apply quality gates

  • Define minimum checks for requirements, security, tests, review, and traceability.
  • Know when to pause, escalate, or request human design review.
  • Make exceptions visible and time-bound.

Sustain adoption

  • Assign ownership for templates, support, reporting, and playbook updates.
  • Use team champions and community sessions to share patterns.
  • Review outcomes and friction at a regular cadence.

Team operating model

Before work starts

  • Problem and outcome are clear.
  • Stakeholders, constraints, and risk owners are known.
  • Story meets the team's definition of ready.

While work is in progress

  • Artifacts stay linked as the plan changes.
  • Agent output is reviewed at agreed checkpoints.
  • Tests and security checks shape the implementation.

Before work is accepted

  • Acceptance evidence is complete and reviewable.
  • Known limitations and exceptions are recorded.
  • Lessons are fed back into templates and team practice.

Metrics and governance

Suggested measures

  • Teams with a named SDD owner and published playbook.
  • Stories with specification, plan, task, test, and review evidence.
  • Time from question raised to decision recorded.
  • Recurring support issues and time to resolution.
  • Quality or rework trends compared with the baseline.

Governance rules

  • Use approved repositories, templates, tools, and data-handling practices.
  • Keep humans accountable for requirements, design, security, and release decisions.
  • Make exceptions explicit, justified, owned, and reviewed.
  • Retire metrics that do not lead to a useful action.
Facilitator emphasis

Operationalization is not “more process.” It is the minimum shared structure that makes AI-assisted work understandable, reviewable, and repeatable across people and teams. Keep the playbook short enough to use during delivery.

Required deliverables and closeout gate

Publish by August 28

  • Team SDD playbook with ceremony map and quality gates.
  • SDD - Agent Squad operating agreement and escalation path.
  • Metrics definition with baseline, owners, cadence, and actions.
  • 30-day improvement backlog and community-of-practice schedule.

Program is operational when

  • Teams can use the workflow without relying on a single individual.
  • Support channels and office hours are active.
  • Leaders can see adoption evidence and meaningful blockers.
  • Feedback has a path into the standards and training materials.
