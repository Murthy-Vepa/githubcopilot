02 / Shared outcomes

From prompt to process.
From output to evidence.

Consistent delivery

Teams use a shared sequence: problem, specification, plan, tasks, implementation, tests, review, and evidence.

Human accountability

People remain accountable for requirements, design, security, testing, acceptance, and release decisions.

Traceable learning

Artifacts stay connected so another developer can understand what changed, why it changed, and what was checked.

Copilot and agents support the engineering system. They do not replace problem definition, judgment, testing, or review.Operating principle for every week
Week 1 | July 27 - July 31

Set the standard.

Give every participating team a shared operating model before tools and workflows are introduced at scale.

Intent→Requirements→Specification→Review points

By Friday

  • SDD process and expectations are approved.
  • Required use and exceptions are documented.
  • Templates and traceability expectations are published.
  • Support channels and office hours are active.
Week 1 / Working sessions

Start with a usable story,
not a code request.

Working exercise

Turn a vague request such as “improve the claims search experience” into a problem statement with users, outcomes, constraints, risks, data considerations, and acceptance evidence.

Readiness gate

  • Participants can access required tools and repositories.
  • The selected story is low-risk and bounded.
  • The team can explain SDD stages and review points.
  • No unresolved security or data-handling blocker remains.
Deliverables: team charter, problem statement, readiness checklist, and open questions.
Week 2 | August 3 - August 7

Build the foundation.

Give teams more preparation time across Fundamentals, AI-Assisted Development, Copilot Agents, and SDD concepts before introducing focused Spec Kit work.

Participants should leave Week 2 confident, curious, and ready to ask better questions in Week 3.
1

Learn the fundamentals.
Build a responsible Copilot mental model and understand quality boundaries.

2

Practice AI-Assisted Development.
Turn ideas into bounded requirements, prompts, and useful small experiments.

3

Explore agents and SDD.
Understand delegation, review checkpoints, and the path from intent to evidence.

Week 2 / Preparation lab

Prepare the story
before the stages.

Clarify the outcome

Use Copilot to identify missing information, stakeholders, constraints, and the user outcome behind the request.

Bound the slice

Choose a small story that can support a focused Spec Kit walkthrough without becoming a full delivery sprint.

Check readiness

Confirm access, support paths, SDD vocabulary, and the questions the team wants to answer in Week 3.

Readiness signal: each participant can use the approved tools and explain the purpose of the Week 3 Spec Kit workshop.
Week 3 | August 10 - August 14

Explore the path
before operationalizing it.

Spend only one to two days on GitHub Spec Kit stages, then introduce the SDD - Agent Squad roles, handoffs, examples, and questions that Week 4 will operationalize.

Intent→Specify→Plan→Handoff→Review

Focused workshop outcomes

  • Map the Spec Kit stages to the team's SDD workflow.
  • Review a specification and plan for assumptions, constraints, and scope.
  • Explore Agent Squad roles, inputs, outputs, and checkpoints.
  • Capture questions for the Week 4 operating review.
Week 3 / Agent Squad session

Make the roles
easy to understand.

Days 3-5

  • Meet the SDD - Agent Squad specialist roles and responsibilities.
  • Trace inputs, outputs, handoffs, checkpoints, and escalation paths.
  • Walk through representative team scenarios and human approvals.
  • Capture blockers, exceptions, and operating questions.

Ready for Week 4

  • Team understands the Agent Squad model without treating it as fully operational.
  • Spec Kit scope stayed within one to two focused days.
  • Operating-model questions have owners and next actions.
  • Teams are ready to define ceremonies, quality gates, and ownership.
Week 4 | August 17 - August 28

Make it routine.

Make SDD - Agent Squad dependable by embedding the workflow in ceremonies, quality gates, ownership, support, and measurable improvement.

Operationalization is the minimum shared structure that makes AI-assisted work understandable, reviewable, and repeatable.
1

Put SDD in the calendar.
Map artifacts and decisions to refinement, planning, stand-ups, demos, and retrospectives.

2

Coordinate specialist work.
Clarify Agent Squad roles, handoffs, checkpoints, and escalation paths.

3

Measure the practice.
Choose useful adoption, quality, flow, and support signals.

Week 4 / Operating review

Govern without
creating noise.

Quality gates

  • Requirements are clear and reviewable.
  • Security, privacy, accessibility, and testing checks are defined.
  • Exceptions are explicit, justified, owned, and time-bound.
  • Humans remain accountable for design and release decisions.

Useful measures

Teams with an SDD owner100%
Stories with connected evidence100%
Support issues with an actionTracked
03 / The operating model

Keep the workflow
visible at every stage.

Before work starts

  • Problem and outcome are clear.
  • Stakeholders, constraints, and risks are known.
  • Story meets the team's definition of ready.

While work is in progress

  • Artifacts stay linked as the plan changes.
  • Agent output is reviewed at checkpoints.
  • Tests and security checks shape implementation.

Before work is accepted

  • Acceptance evidence is complete.
  • Known limitations are recorded.
  • Lessons feed back into team practice.
04 / Closeout

Leave the rollout
stronger than it started.

By August 28

  • Team SDD playbook is published.
  • Agent Squad operating agreement is active.
  • Metrics have an owner, cadence, baseline, and action threshold.
  • Community of practice and support model are scheduled.

Next 30 days

  • Review adoption evidence and recurring blockers.
  • Refresh templates from real team learning.
  • Retire measures that do not lead to action.
  • Share patterns across teams and delivery partners.
The destination is not “more AI.” It is a clearer, more traceable, more responsible way to deliver.
